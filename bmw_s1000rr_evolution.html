Evolution Timeline

Major milestones in the RR story.

2009

The beginning

BMW enters the modern superbike class with the first production S 1000 RR, bringing BMW's racing technology to a road-legal litre bike.

2012–2014

Early development

BMW continues refining the RR with updates to performance, electronics, chassis and styling across the early model years.

2015–2018

Sharper and smarter

The RR receives further technical development as BMW continues improving the engine, chassis, rider aids and overall package.

2019

A new generation

A major redesign introduces a new-generation RR with a more compact engine, revised chassis and a significantly updated electronics package.

2023

More performance

The 2023 RR is rated at 210 hp and 113 Nm, with further development of the engine, chassis, electronics and optional M components.

2025+

Aerodynamic evolution

BMW continues developing the RR with redesigned aerodynamics, including revised winglets and bodywork aimed at increasing aerodynamic performance.

Technology

The RR's development has been about more than engine power.

Electronics

Modern rider-assistance systems help manage acceleration, braking and traction depending on equipment and riding mode.

Aerodynamics

Later-generation bodywork and winglets are designed to improve high-speed aerodynamic performance.

Chassis

BMW has continually developed the frame, suspension and braking package to balance agility with stability.
